# The Sunday review

Re-reading old notes on Sunday evening is the only review habit that ever stuck. Twenty minutes, a pot of tea, no editing. I just read.

The trick was dropping the checklist. A review with twelve steps is a chore, and I skip chores.

What I notice most: I write the same idea every few months and forget I already had it.

## The routine

1. Make tea
2. Open *All notes* and start from the oldest
3. Read. **Do not edit.**

> If a note still surprises me, it earns a ==highlight==.

- [x] This week
- [ ] Next week
