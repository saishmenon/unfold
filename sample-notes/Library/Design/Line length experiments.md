# Line length experiments

![Three column widths](columns.svg)

## Width

Tested three column widths on the same essay. At ninety characters per line I kept losing my place when my eyes travelled back to the left margin. Sixty characters felt slow at first, then restful.

## Spacing

Line spacing matters as much as width. With tight spacing the neighbouring lines blur together. 1.7 was the first setting where I stopped re-reading lines.

## Wide screens

Narrow columns look wasteful on a wide screen. They are not. The empty space is doing a job.

---

### Settings I kept

1. Width: `62` characters
2. Line spacing: `1.7`
3. Alignment: **left**, never justified

> Somewhere between *45 and 75* characters is the old rule of thumb.
