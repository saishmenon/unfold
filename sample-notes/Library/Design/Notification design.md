# Notification design

Badges and red dots borrow attention and never pay it back. A streak counter turns a habit into a debt.

Quiet defaults: nothing interrupts unless I asked for it today. If an app needs a badge to be remembered, it is not useful enough yet.

The best reminder is the work itself, left where I will see it.

## What I turned off

- ~~Streak counters~~
- ~~Unread counts~~
- Weekly *summary* emails

### What stays

1. **Calendar** alerts
2. Messages from ==people==, not apps

> Attention is the only budget I can't top up.
