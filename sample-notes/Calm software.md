# Calm software

A calm app asks for nothing until I need it. No badges, no streaks, no red dots. The screen should feel like a quiet room with good light.

## Reading comfort

Long lines tire my eyes. When a line runs past about sixty characters I lose my place on the way back to the left margin, so I want narrow columns and generous line spacing.

## Finding things

I keep coming back to folders. Every time I sort notes into folders I spend more time filing than writing, and I still can't find anything a month later.
